# VentureProof research protocol

VentureProof is designed to help founders improve ideas, not prematurely kill them.

## Decision philosophy
- BUILD means the evidence supports focused execution; it is not a guarantee.
- PIVOT means the opportunity has signal but a material assumption or positioning needs to change.
- PAUSE & IMPROVE means the current evidence is too weak for significant investment. The founder receives strengths, weaknesses, experiments and a proposed modification.
- INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE means the system does not have enough independent evidence to make a responsible market claim.

## Evidence standard
Every external evidence item retains a URL, source/provider, retrieval context, polarity and strength. The UI separates observed evidence from hypotheses and projected impact.

## Improvement loop
1. Identify the strongest and weakest dimensions.
2. Explain the weakness in plain language.
3. Propose a concrete modification to the offer, customer, channel or monetization model.
4. Attach supporting/contradicting evidence where available.
5. Show projected before/after dimension scores as hypotheses, never facts.
6. Give a small validation experiment to test whether the improvement actually works.

## Research providers
Exa and Tavily are normalized into a common evidence interface. Firecrawl, DataForSEO, SerpAPI and Perplexity can be added as additional independent adapters. If live credentials are unavailable, the product must explicitly say that evidence is unavailable and must never fabricate market proof.
