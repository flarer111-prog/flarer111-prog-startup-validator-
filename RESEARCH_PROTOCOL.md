# Research protocol

A credible report triangulates independent evidence across problem, demand, market size, competitive position, monetization, acquisition feasibility and structural risk. Research should prioritize behavior and source quality over opinions. Search intent, customer voice, competitor traction and willingness-to-pay are stronger signals than compliments or generic AI summaries.

Every evidence item should retain URL, provider/source type, retrieval time, polarity, strength and the claim it supports. If evidence is missing, the product must show **INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE**, not manufacture confidence.

The current adapters support Exa and Tavily through environment variables. Firecrawl, DataForSEO, SerpAPI and Perplexity belong behind the same normalized evidence interface.
